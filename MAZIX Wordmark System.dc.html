Twenty wordmarks. The name is the asset; the X is the signature.
Consulting firms do not have symbols — they have names set with total conviction. This exploration treats MAZIX the same way: four directions, five wordmarks each, all in Obsidian on Warm Paper, with Intelligence Blue used on one letter at most.

RECOMMENDATION
MAZIX
Concept 16 — Plate. A cut plate at cap height, set against Geist Medium at −0.045em with four drawn corrections.
The wordmark leads
As the brief requires, the name is the asset. The plate is a companion sized to cap height, never taller — it can be dropped entirely on covers and stage backdrops without weakening the identity.
It argues the positioning
Intelligence Engineering is subtractive: you remove until only the necessary structure remains. Nothing was added to make this X — material was taken away, precisely.
It is the opposite of AI branding
AI identity is additive — glow, gradient, particles. A heavy, opaque, cut plate looks manufactured rather than rendered. That distinction is the entire sale.
It survives every surface
Self-inverting, so one geometry serves both modes. Embosses, engraves and laser-cuts as drawn. A filled square is the most legible object a 16px tab can hold.
